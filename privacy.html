Privacy

The TScrambler extension and userscript collect nothing and send nothing anywhere.

  • Descrambling happens on your computer, in your browser.
  • Share codes you enter are saved in your browser's own storage, per channel, and never leave it.
  • The extension reads Twitch pages and Twitch's video servers only to descramble the video you are watching.
  • No analytics, no accounts, no servers.
